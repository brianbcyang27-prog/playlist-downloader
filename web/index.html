Speed & stability

3–4 parallel downloads is the sweet spot. YouTube throttles bursts, so the app retries transient failures with backoff instead of giving up — that matters when you queue a few thousand tracks. Use Test mode to check that a big playlist resolves correctly before committing to the download.

Access & networking

Spotify credentials are free from developer.spotify.com and give accurate album metadata. Without them the app still works using the public playlist page.
